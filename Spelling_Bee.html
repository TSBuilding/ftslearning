The Soft C Rule!
A real rule you can use to read new words. Look at the letter after the C.
c
+e i y
Soft C = sounds like /s/.
When C is followed by e, i, or y.
cent · city · cycle
c
+a o u
Hard C = sounds like /k/.
When C is followed by a, o, u, or a consonant.
cat · cup · clock
🎯 The good news: this rule is reliable! Once you know it, you can work out lots of new words on your own — just look at what letter comes after the C.
