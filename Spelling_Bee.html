The 'dge' Rule!
'dge' makes the /j/ sound — and there's a rule for when to use it.
dge
Use dge right after a short vowel at the end of a word.
badge · hedge · bridge · dodge
ge
Use just ge after a long vowel or a consonant.
cage · huge · large
🎯 Remember 'ck'? This works the same way! Short vowels like a quick, chunky ending — that's why they get the extra 'd' in 'dge', just like short vowels get 'ck'.
