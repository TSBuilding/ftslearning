The 'ear' Sound!
When you hear /ear/ at the end of a word, it's usually spelled e-a-r.
ear
The three letters e, a, r work as a team to make the /ear/ sound.
near · dear · year · fear
Listen for the /ear/ sound — the spelling almost always follows!
